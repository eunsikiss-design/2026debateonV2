## Rules
Background #0a0e17; surfaces #0f131d/#141a26; cyan #00f2fe; violet #8b5cf6; translucent borders. Glow is reserved for current input and main actions. Radius 8–12px. All actionable touch targets at least 44px. Plus Jakarta Sans with Pretendard/Noto Sans KR fallback, JetBrains Mono for HUD labels only. Reduced-motion supported. Never equate a completed input with a validated argument.

390px: stacked learning modules and four fixed tabs. Tablet: two-column workspaces. Desktop: wider editorial workspace, with primary writing to the right and topic/context to the left. Battle remains a separate route, accessible from every header.
