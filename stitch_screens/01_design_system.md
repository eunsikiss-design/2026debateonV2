## Brand & Style

This design system establishes an intellectual, highly structured, yet energetic learning environment tailored for Korean secondary students engaging with Integrated Social Studies (통합사회), debate preparation, and thesis-driven essay composition.

### Core Philosophy
The visual and interaction paradigm merges **Academic Rigor** with **Empathetic AI Guidance**. Rather than feeling like a sterile testing portal or a gamified distraction, the interface mimics a sophisticated digital agora: clean editorial layouts that encourage deep focus, paired with active, luminous accents that demystify logical fallacies, guide argument construction (Claim-Reason-Evidence), and reward critical discourse.

### Visual Dialect
- **Modern Intellectual Minimalism with Tactile Precision:** A crisp, high-clarity canvas characterized by clean porcelain surfaces, quiet slate borders, and deliberate typographic rhythm.
- **Dynamic Socratic Feedback:** Bright, deliberate chromatic signals (electric cyan, mint, and warm amber) demarcate AI coaching interventions, logical scoring, counter-argument prompts, and audio recording fidelity.
- **Atmosphere:** Scholarly, encouraging, articulate, and trustworthy. The interface evokes the confidence of a distinguished academic mentor combined with the clarity of a modern collaborative workspace.

## Colors

The palette balances foundational academic authority with sharp, intentional signals for dialectical learning and real-time AI critique.

### Color Roles

- **Primary (`#1E40AF` — Scholastic Deep Indigo):** Anchor color used for primary CTAs, active debate headers, major navigation states, and authoritative structural framing. Communicates focus, intellectual integrity, and discipline.
- **Secondary (`#06B6D4` — Electric Cyan / Synthesis Mint):** Reserved for active logic threads, AI synthesis indicators, thesis validation milestones, and voice wave visualization active states.
- **Tertiary (`#F59E0B` — Socratic Amber):** Used for critical prompts, logical friction notices (e.g., fallacies, ungrounded claims), rubric scoring markers, and constructive feedback callouts.
- **Neutral (`#64748B` — Slate Matrix):** Drives typographic balance, borders, and structured compartmentalization across the porcelain canvas (`#F8FAFC` slate-50 base with `#FFFFFF` card surfaces).

### Semantic & Contextual Variants
- **Premise / Pro-Argument Mint (`#10B981`):** Applied to affirming points, valid citations, and structural strengths.
- **Rebuttal / Counter Coral (`#F97316`):** Dedicated to opposition cues, counter-arguments, and challenge mode cards.
- **Surface Elevation:** Backgrounds rest on `#F8FAFC`, stepping up to `#FFFFFF` for working cards, with outer boundary strokes held strictly at `#E2E8F0`.

## Typography

The typographic hierarchy is optimized for dual Latin and Hangul script legibility. English alphanumeric markers (rubric scores, unit codes, time trackers) render in **Plus Jakarta Sans**, while intensive Korean reading passages, curriculum prompts, and multi-paragraph student essays render through the high-clarity humanist grotesque **Noto Sans** (with Pretendard parity).

### Editorial Rules
- **Line Heights for Dense Reading:** Body levels maintain generous vertical rhythm (`1.6` to `1.7` ratio) to mitigate reading fatigue during long historical and ethical source analyses.
- **Argument Scaffolding Font:** `code-argument` provides clean monospaced utility for formal debate round timing, logical syllogism tags (`P1`, `P2`, `C`), and rebuttal token markers.

## Layout & Spacing

The spatial architecture balances editorial immersion with split-screen comparative debate workflows.

### Grid System
- **Desktop (1200px+):** 12-column dynamic grid with `gutter: 1.5rem` and outer page margins capped at `margin: 2.5rem`. Enables side-by-side workspace: 7 columns dedicated to the primary essay editor or debate transcription, 5 columns to real-time AI rubric scoring, source material, and counter-perspective stacks.
- **Tablet (768px – 1199px):** 8-column layout. The AI assistant and argumentation scaffolding tuck into a persistent side drawer or collapsible bottom sheet.
- **Mobile (< 768px):** 4-column fluid layout with `margin: 1rem`. Argument components, audio timers, and AI critique pills collapse into stacked contextual feeds.

### Spatial Rhythm
Inner card component padding follows standard rhythmic scale:
- `space-xs` (4px) & `space-sm` (8px) govern tag clusters, pill badges, and compact metric icons.
- `space-md` (16px) controls form field internal padding and list item separation.
- `space-lg` (24px) establishes standard card interior padding.
- `space-xl` (40px) demarcates major unit modules and debate phase shifts.

## Elevation & Depth

This design system uses a tactile, low-elevation layering system to keep students grounded in their cognitive workspace rather than distracted by aggressive drop shadows.

### Depth Hierarchy
1. **Base Floor (Canvas):** `#F8FAFC` (Slate-50) tinted matte canvas.
2. **Layer 0 (Working Cards):** `#FFFFFF` surfaces bounded by a crisp `1px solid #E2E8F0` border. No drop shadow in default resting state to maintain clean structure.
3. **Layer 1 (Focused Input & Argument Cards):** `#FFFFFF` with a directional micro-shadow: `box-shadow: 0 4px 12px -2px rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.06), 0 2px 4px -1px rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.04)`.
4. **Layer 2 (Floating Coaching Bubbles & Overlays):** `#FFFFFF` backed by subtle Indigo/Cyan ambient dispersion: `box-shadow: 0 12px 28px -6px rgba(30, 64, 175, 0.08), 0 8px 10px -4px rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.03)`.
5. **Active Audio Recording Floating Bar:** Anchored to bottom viewports with frosted glass backing (`backdrop-filter: blur(12px)`) at `rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.88)` and a border stroke of `rgba(6, 182, 212, 0.3)`.

## Shapes

A friendly, modern `roundedness: 2` (16px base on cards and main surfaces) removes academic austerity while maintaining functional utility.

### Geometric Scale
- **Base Surfaces & Argument Blocks (`rounded-2xl` / 16px):** Debate perspective cards, essay writing containers, and AI feedback panels.
- **Interactive Controls (`rounded-xl` / 12px):** Form inputs, buttons, and structured dropdown selectors.
- **Meta Badges & Audio Wave Containers (`rounded-full` / 9999px):** Unit markers, status badges, timers, and recording controller pills.

## Components

### 1. Buttons & Action Controllers
- **Primary CTA (Debate Submission / Thesis Check):** Solid Deep Indigo (`#1E40AF`) with white text, `rounded-xl`, subtle scale transition on hover (`1.02`), and active press depth (`0.98`).
- **AI Action Secondary:** Ghost style with Electric Cyan tint (`#ECFEFF`), border `1px solid #06B6D4`, text in `#0E7490`.
- **Destructive / Rebuttal Dismissal:** Soft Coral tint (`#FFF7ED`), text `#C2410C`.

### 2. Social Studies Unit Badges (단원 태그)
- High-contrast micro-pills (`rounded-full`, horizontal padding: 10px, vertical: 4px).
- **Unit 1: 인권과 정의 (Human Rights & Justice):** Soft Violet tint background (`#F3E8FF`), text `#6B21A8`.
- **Unit 2: 시장 경제와 금융 (Market Economy):** Soft Emerald background (`#ECFDF5`), text `#065F46`.
- **Unit 3: 환경 윤리와 지속가능성 (Environmental Ethics):** Soft Teal background (`#F0FDFA`), text `#115E59`.
- **Unit 4: 평화와 세계화 (Peace & Globalization):** Soft Blue background (`#EFF6FF`), text `#1E40AF`.

### 3. Argument Structure Cards (Claim-Reason-Evidence)
- Modular cards with left border accentuation:
  - **주장 (Claim):** 3px border-l `#1E40AF`, background `#FFFFFF`.
  - **근거 (Reasoning):** 3px border-l `#06B6D4`, background `#FFFFFF`.
  - **증거 자료 (Evidence / Data):** 3px border-l `#F59E0B`, background `#FFFFFF`.
- Features an inline citation drawer and drag handle for structural reordering during debate prep.

### 4. AI Coaching Callout Bubbles & Score Metrics
- Asymmetric card with an amber/cyan glowing badge indicator ("소크라 AI 진단").
- Displays real-time critique on fallacies (e.g., 성급한 일반화의 오류 / Ad Hominem alerts).
- Integrated circular micro-gauge showing logical coherence percentage (0-100%).

### 5. Audio Recording Waveform Bar
- Floating bottom pill container (`#FFFFFF` glass with subtle cyan border).
- Houses dual state: a pulsating live mic icon, timer display (`code-argument`), and 24-bar audio visualizer utilizing alternating heights driven by primary Indigo and secondary Cyan gradients.

### 6. Logic Competency Radar Chart
- Embedded inside assessment summaries: Five-axis polygon tracking **논리성 (Logic)**, **비판적 사고 (Critical Thinking)**, **어휘/표현력 (Diction)**, **반박 대응력 (Rebuttal Capability)**, and **윤리적 다각도 분석 (Multiperspective Analysis)**.
- Rendered with Cyan-tinted fills (`rgba(6, 182, 212, 0.2)`) over crisp `#E2E8F0` grid web lines.

### 7. Form Inputs & Essay Writing Zone
- Auto-growing text area with distraction-free padding (`space-lg`), subtle line numbers, active word/character counts, and real-time spell-and-logic lint underlines.
