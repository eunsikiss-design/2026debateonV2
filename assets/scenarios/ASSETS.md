# Scenario illustrations

Created 2026-09-25 with native image generation. WebP conversion preserves composition. These are fictional thematic illustrations, not textbook figures or actual students. No student data or textbook scans were submitted.

Art direction: panoramic editorial illustration for a Korean high-school social studies app; dark navy and muted teal, warm natural light, two equally dignified protagonists, no written labels or logos. Character facts and competing positions remain editable text, separate from the decorative image.

Five images are reused by unit. Housing uses justice. They are not 40 individually commissioned scenes and do not automatically change when a teacher edits the story.

## rights.webp

Scene prompt: Two Korean high-school students with different wishes regarding an old online video: one holding a phone protectively, the other holding a small family photograph; a subtle urban neighborhood and digital window motifs. Respectful equal emphasis, no aggressor, no victim caricature.

## justice.webp

Scene prompt: Two adult Korean residents conversing beside an urban apartment neighborhood, one holding a house key and a work bag, one holding a household budget notebook; bus stop, modest housing and public buildings in background. Both equally dignified.

## economy.webp

Scene prompt: A small shop owner and a young consumer discussing a locally produced item beside a neighborhood market, subtle delivery crates, workers and green energy in background. Both equally dignified.

## world.webp

Scene prompt: Two young people from different cultural backgrounds conversing around a globe in a civic exchange space, a harbor and distant landscape in background, cooperation and different perspectives. Both equally dignified.

## future.webp

Scene prompt: A younger and an older Korean citizen discussing a future neighborhood, with river, renewable energy, trees, public transport and homes in the background. Equal emphasis on both people.
